# Findings and remediation

## Evidence thresholds

| Case | Category | Action |
|---|---|---|
| An applicable mandatory field is demonstrably absent from a complete, readable SDS, including any permitted location or cross-reference. | Confirmed legal non-compliance, after verifying the provision and applicability. | Identify the required field, destination subsection, authoritative value source and related updates. |
| The SDS lists ingredients, but the reviewer lacks the full formulation and cannot establish whether another constituent must be reported. | Open issue requiring verification. | Request the composition or validated disclosure assessment and identify the relevant criterion to check. |
| The draft uses unfamiliar wording, but its meaning is accurate and there is no demonstrated breach. | Recommendation, if improving clarity or satisfying a supplied internal policy. | Propose a clearer equivalent or reference the applicable approved wording. |
| A dated enforcement report flags a pattern, but the applicable law cannot be verified. | Open issue requiring verification if a concrete issue was observed. Otherwise record the check as unassessed. | Identify the missing binding source and what in the SDS needs checking. |
| Two sections disagree about a safety-relevant property and the underlying measurement is unavailable. | Open issue requiring verification. | Prioritise resolving the discrepancy and correct all dependent sections from the verified measurement. |

In a document-only review, absence of upstream evidence is not evidence that a supplier failed to act. Distinguish observed omission, contradiction, illegible extraction, unverified applicability and a supported reason for non-applicability.

## Finding record

For each finding give:

- **ID and location** with SDS subsection, PDF page or printed page as available, and linked locations.
- **Observation and evidence** with the exact relevant passage or the specific missing field and how absence was checked. Give product evidence references when used.
- **Basis and applicability** with a binding pinpoint clause, applicable date/version and source for a legal finding. For an open issue, identify which element is unresolved. For a recommendation, cite the guidance, internal policy or practical reason.
- **Priority** as high, medium or low, based on consequences for safety, supply or usability and the order in which fixes are needed. Priority does not establish legal status.
- **Correction and verification** with what to change, where, the source or owner of any missing data, related sections and a check to confirm the correction.

## Coverage

Use `ASSESSED`, `NEEDS_EVIDENCE`, `NOT_APPLICABLE` (with reason), or `NOT_CHECKED` for coverage. If assessed, link to any finding IDs. In the REF-11 matrix, interpret `FAIL_VERIFIED` only as a confirmed legal finding under this skill's threshold. Treat suspected issues as open issues, and show the source supporting the scope of any passed check. A REF-11 check can be split across document and supply evidence. Report the limits of each separately. Avoid aggregate compliance scores.
