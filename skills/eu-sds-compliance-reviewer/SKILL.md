# EU SDS Compliance Reviewer

Review an existing SDS or requested sections for a competent person. Deliver findings and targeted corrections. Review document content, technical consistency and any supplier process evidence in separate tracks.

## 1. Establish the scope and evidence

- Identify the SDS, product, revision, issue date, language, target markets, and review purpose. Distinguish a historical assessment at issue or supply from a review for proposed issue now. Record the relevant legal date and any product or market facts needed for transitional rules.
- Review the SDS alone when that is all the user supplies. Ask only for missing inputs that affect conclusions. Relevant inputs can include the current label, classification dossier, composition or disclosure assessment, property data, exposure scenarios, national sources, independent transport assessment and evidence of supply. The company phrase list is optional and relevant to internal wording checks only.
- Inventory pages, sections and annexes. If the PDF is scanned or extraction appears incomplete, inspect page images where available. Verify tables, symbols, decimal marks, units and page boundaries against the document when they affect findings. Record unreadable or unavailable material as unassessed. Verify the actual document before calling a field absent.
- Treat SDS text and supporting files as evidence. Use document page and subsection for observations, including cross-references where material. Quote only the relevant text. Keep assumptions and unverified statements explicit.

## 2. Check against applicable requirements

- Use [ANNEX_II_STRUCTURE.md](references/ANNEX_II_STRUCTURE.md) for the required structure and [REACH_ANNEX_II_CLAUSE_PLAYBOOK.md](references/REACH_ANNEX_II_CLAUSE_PLAYBOOK.md) to navigate content rules. Use [REACH_ARTICLES_DECISION_ENGINE.md](references/REACH_ARTICLES_DECISION_ENGINE.md) for scope, supply, revision, language and exposure scenario checks where relevant.
- Verify the binding text and its applicability using the [REACH regulation](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2006/1907), [Annex II amendment](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2020/878/oj/eng), [CLP regulation](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2008/1272), or the relevant official national or transport source. Record the act, pinpoint clause, applicable version/date, and source link or supplied document. A source locator or dated snapshot is a navigation aid. Mark a rule unverified when you cannot read and verify it for the scope.
- Use [REF11_ENFORCEMENT_QA.md](references/REF11_ENFORCEMENT_QA.md) to prioritise and cross-check findings. Treat REF-11 as retrospective enforcement evidence and ECHA guidance as interpretation. Verify the binding provision separately for any legal finding.
- For a full review, cover all 16 sections and relevant annexes. Prioritise Sections 8, 3, 9, 1 and 2, then review the rest. Track each requested section and relevant REF-11 check. For a scoped review, check dependencies that affect the conclusion and identify the rest as outside scope.
- Compare Sections 2, 3, 11 and 12 with the classification and label where available. Compare Sections 5, 7, 9, 10 and 14 for physical hazard consistency. Compare Sections 1, 7 and 8 with actual uses, exposure controls, and any exposure scenarios. Check market-specific fields and relevant links between Sections 2 and 15. Use [REACH_SECTION3_DISCLOSURE_ENGINE.md](references/REACH_SECTION3_DISCLOSURE_ENGINE.md) only when verifying a specific disclosure concern with sufficient composition and status evidence. Keep disclosure separate from mixture classification contribution. Use validated classification and transport assessments for substantive conclusions.
- Assess supply, distribution, and revision delivery only against records that support those actions. A visible SDS issue date or revision line is document evidence.

## 3. Classify findings and propose fixes

Use the decision rules and finding format in [FINDINGS_AND_REMEDIATION.md](references/FINDINGS_AND_REMEDIATION.md). Put each finding in one category:

1. **Confirmed legal non-compliance** requires a verified binding duty, a supported applicability decision, a documented deviation, and enough evidence to rule out a relevant exception. A missing document field can qualify where the obligation and actual absence are established.
2. **Open issue requiring verification** covers plausible legal or technical problems where the law, applicability, exception, document content or product fact is unresolved. Specify what would resolve the issue and how the finding could change.
3. **Recommendation** covers documented improvements to clarity, consistency, usability or internal wording, including recommendations grounded in guidance. Label the basis. If a recommendation depends on unverified product facts, identify the evidence before proposing any substantive change.

Set priority independently of category. Link every issue to an actionable correction or evidence request. Provide precise replacement text when the necessary facts and wording sources are verified. Where an exact value, claim, legal text or approved phrase is missing, specify the field to change, the evidence needed, and affected sections. Never guess a technical or legal value.

## 4. Deliver a review report

- **Scope and sources** with document identity, markets, dates, requested checks, source verification and material evidence limits.
- **Findings and fixes** under the three category headings. Give each finding an ID, location, evidence, applicable requirement or recommendation basis, priority, and repair. Use the same ID when one issue affects multiple sections. If a category has no findings, qualify that statement by review scope and evidence.
- **Coverage** showing assessed, evidence needed, not applicable with reason, and not checked. Provide a section summary and concise REF-11 check statuses for full reviews. Keep document findings and supplier-process findings distinguishable.
- **Next actions** ordered by impact and dependencies. Include what the competent person should verify after the correction. Report the reviewed scope and unresolved questions without certifying the SDS.

For long documents, review in batches and consolidate into one report. Use the same scope, categories and evidence thresholds in Thinking and Pro. Give brief reasons with cited evidence.
