# Source hierarchy and date controls for writing EU SDSs

## Supplied v4 source documents (unchanged)
- `sources/REACH_1907_2006_consolidated_EN_2026-06-22.pdf`: 603-page non-authentic consolidated text, header `22.06.2026 — 069.001`. Article 31 pp.39–42, REACH Annex II pp.117–152, Part B headings pp.150–152.
- `sources/ECHA_Guidance_on_Compilation_of_SDS_v4_Dec2020.pdf`: ECHA compilation guidance v4.0, December 2020; interpretation and section-writing workflow, **not binding law** and cannot override subsequent amendments.
- `sources/ECHA_REF11_SDS_Enforcement_Report_Dec2024.pdf`: retrospective REF-11 inspection report, useful to spot common SDS quality failures; **not a legal requirement** or representative EU-wide noncompliance estimate.

A consolidated text can lag later amendments. For any consequential requirement, check the **applicable consolidated legislative text and authentic amendatory instruments**, effective/application/transitional dates, current CLP annexes and Member State requirements for the actual SDS issue date.

## Core authoring legal references
- REACH Regulation (EC) No 1907/2006, especially Annex II Parts A and B, applicable version; Article 31 only where it informs content, issue/revision details or exposure scenarios.
- Commission Regulation (EU) 2020/878 amending Annex II (current baseline SDS format; historical transition ended in 2022).
- CLP Regulation (EC) No 1272/2008, applicable version, for actual Section 2 classification/label content and ingredient-specific classification fields.
- CLP additional hazard classes under Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2023/707 (date/market-placement/transitional facts matter).
- Regulation (EU) 2024/2865 as amended: check staged application dates; do not assume its original dates are still valid.
- Member State OELs, emergency arrangements, language and specific local requirements, and independently established transport/waste inputs.

## Traceability fields
For each legal or factual claim record `source title | source tier (law/guidance/report/fact/phrase) | date/revision | pinpoint clause or page actually examined | product fact | output implication | unresolved uncertainty`.

**Do not fabricate a pinpoint legal reference.** If the law was not actually checked, say `LEGAL VERSION / CLAUSE VERIFICATION PENDING`. A legal content requirement does not authorise inventing an approved phrase; an approved phrase does not establish a product fact.

Final issue of an SDS requires a trained competent-person check, valid target-country language and accurate sources. A source date in this package alone is not proof of ongoing legal currency.
