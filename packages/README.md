# SDS_Author independent Gemini skill packages

The packages here are **separate skills**, designed to be installed or zipped independently.

| Package | Use for | Does not do |
|---|---|---|
| [EU SDS Writer](eu-sds-writer/) | Writing and refining an SDS using validated product data and a designated pre-approved phrase bank. | Classification calculations, SDS grouping, standalone regulatory compliance certification. |
| [EU SDS Compliance Reviewer](eu-sds-reviewer/) | Reviewing an **existing** SDS for proven legal non-compliances, potential legal concerns, nonbinding improvement recommendations and unverified checks; explaining exact corrections and re-reviewing fixes. | New SDS authoring, classification calculations, grouping or regulatory certification. |

Each package contains its own **SKILL.md** and instructions, references, source PDFs and tests. In Gemini, upload the **contents of the chosen package**, keeping that package's SKILL.md at its root. Do not combine their instruction files into one skill.

Both require authoritative, date-appropriate EU/Member State law and verified product evidence. The bundled ECHA guidance and REF-11 enforcement report are nonbinding. Final review and release always belong to a competent person.
